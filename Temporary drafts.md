# "I'm having a moment" (August 2026)
*"When's the last time you did a spacewalk anyway?"* \
A sigh left her lips. If it was up to her, she wouldn't even need the EVA suit anyway, but refusing to take any more risks than necessary, she was already halfway through the dressing procedure as Petra knocked on the wall next to the *Bronze Dart*'s airlock. **"Years, if not decades."** Maria smiled back, barely having put on the lower half of the suit as her eyes looked at her colleague. It's **2099**, and with just a handful of months left before Maria's ascension, she decided to scratch off the last few things on her bucket list while she still had her mortal life. Though in fact, she had always been immortal, she just refused to come to terms with it. \
She averted her eyes from Petra, and looked through the small, circular window on the hatch. It was the only thing separating the two of them from the infinite expanse of the universe, but right now, they were were in orbit of **Oceana**. Pushing her arms through the sleeves of the top half of the EVA suit, Petra smirked, taking the other spacesuit off the wall. They had a convenient white and blue colour to them, the same tint that Moondial had been using for almost all of their spaceship interiors. Despite being a ship built by *Carbine*, the **230** had been docked here for years, serving as one of the only ways of travel between **Cintura** and **Choreos**. **"You're coming along?"** Maria asked in surprise, holding her suit's helmet under her arm as she stood up, watching Petra clumsily and quickly work her way into her own suit. *"I haven't done it in years either..-"* She mumbled back, already over halfway done working herself into her suit. *"This would be a prime opportunity to relive it."*

Before long, the two women had closed the hatch behind them, separating them from the interior of the **Bronze Dart**, and tying themselves to multiple metres of robe, Maria opened the hatch that led them towards the vacuum of space. In an almost endless sea of stars, it only took them a few steps in zero gravity to reach the top of the ship, and with their backs faced to **Oceana**, a deep exhale left both of their lips. For a few minutes, no words were said. **Cintura**—The sun that Raléi had named, was covering the pair in rays of sunshine, and turning around, it became clear as day what one of many recipients of **Cintura**'s light was. The ocean planet of **Oceana**, covered in endless waters. 

Inhaling deeply, Maria closed her eyes, and her hands reached for the clasp on the helmet  that sealed herself away from the vacuum of space, and clipping it open, her headpiece immediately lost all of the air and pressure that was trapped inside, escaping into the void. In an instant, Maria's eyes sparkled in her abnormal tint of blue, green, and pink, and to the concern and amazement of Petra beside her, the two of them were both at a loss for words. Maria's helmet floated off the neckpiece slowly, revealing her hair, the streaks of grey within them, and the slight purple luminescence from her shoulders.
*"You're breaking a lot of laws right now, you know?"* Petra mumbled in disbelief, her mouth agape behind the glass of her own EVA helmet, as she watched Maria's glimmering eyes and hair float in empty space. It should be a sight impossible, and yet.. \
**"Laws in open space don't apply beyond Sol and Choreos, so the EVA protocol also doesn't apply here."** \
*"The laws of physics, Maria."* Petra smirked back, her eyes both looking at her colleague in disbelief and amazement. *"The laws of physics, nature.. the universe.. none of this makes sense, and yet I'm witnessing it right before my very own eyes."* \
Maria smiled, her eyes dead set on the light blue planet before the two. Clouds covered primarily the poles, with a few hurricanes spread throughout the remainder of the Earth-sized planet filled with nothing but water. She fastened her helmet to a clip at her waist and wringed her feet underneath a section of rails aboard the Bronze Dart's roof, and promptly used it to propel herself downwards—Sitting down with her eyes captivated by **Oceana** before her. **"It's a mystery to me too."** Maria admitted with a sigh, the purple glow of her luminescent arms shining through the various vital peeking glasses in her EVA suit. Her halo was anchored behind her back, shimmering in a deep purple that lit up the surface of the otherwise grey Bronze Dart beneath her. Lifting her hand, it was almost as if she bent gravity to her will, golden ripples appearing in the openness of space, before evaporating into the distance almost immediately after. **"But it's beyond choice. This is all I will ever know."** 

Maria looked to her left, seeing that Petra had followed her gaze towards the ocean planet filled to the brim with mystery. The Honour/Glory project had long been postponed, but hadn't been abandoned. It was a matter of time before a more thorough exploration of **Oceana** could go underway—But it would be without Maria. \
*"That's.. going to take some getting used to.."* Petra sighed, her left hand holding tightly onto another rail on top of the small-sized spaceship. Her gaze drooped down to her knees. *"We've been working together for four years. It flew by. Yet there's so many more answers I want to find."* Tears found their way into her eyes, blurring her vision with no gravity to roll them down her cheeks. *"It's.. September, isn't it..? Just four months left.."* \
A golden ripple found its way to Petra's eyes, and by sheer miracle, the blurriness in her eyes vanished as the tears fell down her cheeks, staining her shirt underneath the EVA suit. Looking to her right, she saw Maria's luminescent hand in a pinching motion—literally creating a force of gravity to help Petra's tears fall—As her own eyes were still gazing deeply at the distant storms on **Oceana**. Sniffing, and quietly coughing through a smile, Petra looked back at the same planet before her very own eyes. Even without Maria at her side, an enormous path was still laid out before her feet to walk down. Mapping **Oceana**—even a mere one percent, would deem this decade-long mission a success. *"Let's head back in.."* She chuckled quietly, slowly pulling herself upwards again at the ship's railing. *"Not being able to scratch these itchy tears on my face..—Really annoying."*
# The Alessi Shuttle aftermath (August 2026)

A hundred souls lost, a research vessel lost in another planetary system, and of two, only one severely damaged vessel made it back to **Lumen**. With the Alessi Shuttles being that large, it was impossible to keep it under wraps, and before long, people came knocking at Carbine's doors for answers. What happened to the crews? Why was the **Silver Pike** still lost in the **Cintura** system? Was the **Dvóniks** settlement project cancelled? Questions were asked left and right, some urgent, some confused, some worried. A hundred souls looked to be lost to space. What would Carbine do with that?

The constant flashes from the cameras before her captured nothing but a tired—*exhausted* expression on her face. For over a week, she had continuously read and listened through the black box logs that were aboard the **Silver Pike**, and even though she was practically able to recite the entire hours-long logs by heart at this point, it didn't give her a proper picture of what truly happened. The obvious answer was *"No"*, but Maria lacked the confidence to tell that to the crowd. Right now, they were angered, and would not believe anything that was told to them. She let out another deep sigh before she picked up the paper before her. **"Carbine Industries confirms lost souls aboard the 327, and is actively investigating the lead cause that led to this incident. We know as much as you do, and cannot provide any more information than we already have and know. Our lead goal is to recover as many clues from the 327 as possible, to determine what happened, while we reassess the goal for the Dvóniks project."** \
The crowd lit up, and as Maria herself stood up, they were quick to follow, everyone's question louder than the one asked before, making no question clear for Maria to understand and answer. She took the papers that were at her table and held them underneath her armpit and arm, and facing her colleague, he promptly stood up as well and the two made their way back outside, ignoring the roar from the crowd, the demands from the crowd for answers that they could not provide. The cameras continued to flash, capturing every step they took. Maria only looked down at her gloved hand as she stepped through the doorframe. A young reporter picked up on it. *"—What's the reason for the gloves? Please!"* She asked aloud, her voice barely making it through the ocean of sounds from the other questions being asked that overwhelmed her own, quiet voice. Maria paused, and turned around, facing the young reporter with eyes open wide, followed by a slight, innocent smile. Even Maria's own voice didn't manage to break through the volume of the room, but a lip reader could easily pick out the words she spoke.

# Lúniks, and far, far beyond (August 2026)
## Tau Ceti and Eridani
**Tau Ceti**, as well as **Epsilon Eridani**, are entirely viable solar systems that could host planets that could be habitable, just like *Earth* is, and what *Lúniks* was proven to be in the late **1990**s before the settlement thereof began in the early **2000**s.

While **Moonlight Industries**—through a joint effort between **Moondial** and **Carbine Industries**—lays their focus primarily on **Cintura**'s ocean planet of *Oceana*, Carbine themselves is actively keeping their eyes peeved on Eridani, and **Hammerhead Industries** looks to send another self-sufficient settlement ship to Tau Ceti for research and exploration, too. While Maria's time is running out by **2098**, right in the midst of Moondial's *Project Honour/Glory* and *Project Pond-1*, she is at least more than relieved to see that her lifelong mission of re-igniting the flame for space exploration had been successful after all this time. 

**Hammerhead** is looking to send a ship similar to the *Hail Mary* towards *Tau Ceti e*, a candidate planet that is hard to analyse from both the Solar and Choreos systems, so Hammerhead's solution was to send a crewed flight there to properly analyse the planet in the so-called *Goldilock's Zone*—The habitable zone that Earth and Lúniks find themselves in. Depending on the results of their research, they would log their findings and return to *Shánzhe*, guiding Hammerhead to build the foundation and blueprint for a space station that could orbit the candidate planet, exactly like Moonlight's approach to Oceana, with the resulting space station **Oceana-1**. 

**Moonlight** and **Carbine**, under partial supervision of Maria, continue their successful collaboration, and continue to spread out similar probes through newly-formed Panjit Loops towards neighbouring star systems. While some have stars that are already known not to be stable or warm enough to support life on an orbiting planet in any way, some of these stars still stick out like a sore thumb—They're almost *too* perfect. Such is the case of **Epsilon Eridani**, most specifically *Ægir*, or **Epsilon Eridani b**. While being a planet similar in size to Sol's *Jupiter*, its moons prove to be most interesting, and it could suit as another satellite point which would pave the way to more detailed research within the solar system and neighbouring systems, too.
