# HONOUR/GLORY Drafts
Following the aftermath of the fate of the **Alessi Shuttles** in [[Routine Maintenance - ARC ver.]], both *Maria* and *Cisila* find themselves in a fate similar to that what they had just escaped out of. With the *Silver Pike* left for scrap, and the *Platinum Halberd* undergoing renovation after the incident in the **Cintura System**, the two now find themselves in the same solar system facing a new goal and mission: Researching what the ocean planet *Oceana* can provide for humanity in the near future.

Maria tightens her bonds with *Petra Vilassa*, head of **Moondial**, subsidiary of **Moonlight Industries**—once a rival to Carbine, now a partner. Setting forth a mission and objective with a similar intention, the two combine their forces, and Carbine spearheads Moondial's mission on Oceana. Under supervision of Carbine's Elite, the two Moonlight-designed vessels, **Honour** and **Glory**, are labelled as Carbine's *430* and *431*, before their respective launches in early 2097. With a mission set in stone, and ready for commencement, Maria and Petra observe from station *Oceana-1* as the two enormous vessels descend towards the vast ocean planet.

Aboard the **Honour**, Cisila is only able to watch fate repeat itself before her very eyes once more, as the **Glory** breaks off contact abruptly upon burning through the atmosphere, crash-landing three Blocks east of Pond-1, far off its original destination, but close to the plotted landing site for the **Honour**. But ultimately, the **Honour** is doomed a similar fate, breaking through the atmosphere terribly, and crash-landing in the endless ocean surface, Maria is only able to witness lightning strike twice in such a short time.

### GLORY disappears from the radar (Snippet #1)

An LED on the opposite end of the bridge began to blink, and standing up from her seat, Maria's coat brushed past Petra's chair as she approached it. A quiet beeping became apparent the closer she came to the various monitors. Petra's own *magnum opus* had commenced a few hours ago, with research vessels **Honour** and **Glory** having jumped from Lúniks to Oceana-1, the same space station in orbit of **Oceana** that Maria and Petra were positioned upon. And Oceana was the same planet that these two enormous ships would soon descend upon. Oceana was ‒ like its name suggested ‒ an ocean planet. Apart from a few peaks from underwater mountain ranges poking out of of the endless seas, no habitable land was present anywhere on this Mars-sized planet. \
**"Dr. Vilassa."** Maria spoke quietly under her breath, having frozen in place before the monitor while the beeping had already made her ears go numb, and almost instinctively, the slight luminescence of the Halo already started shining through the multiple layers of fabric on her back. *"Honorifics..?"* Petra replied in surprise, immediately turning towards Maria's direction, recognising the iridescent and luminescent colours on her clothing. Whatever it was, it was no test. *"Maria, is there?-"*
**"Glory's gone."** She simply replied, a deep sigh following her lips as her hands were anchored to the desk. *"Glory's..-?"* Petra muttered dumbfoundedly, immediately turning towards the enormous glass panels at the bridge, giving a clear overview of Oceana in its natural beauty. The light from Cintura, her sun, was facing their backs, making Oceana appear as round and bright as it could possibly be. But while the faint orange flow of *Honour* could still be seen descending to Oceana, the sight of *Glory* had entirely vanished. *"Glory's gone..?!"* \
**"The 410 and 411 are built to transform into something akin to Pond-1 upon landing."** Maria began, facing away from the monitor to once more look at Petra, who was visibly distressed. Lifting up her left hand, memories flashed before her eyes from not even a year ago, when both of the *Alessi Shuttles* had vanished from the **ARC**'s radar in a similar fashion. As the seconds ticked by, the purple glow crept upwards, reaching towards her fingertips, entirely out of her own control. **"It has a 100% success rate from 126 individually performed tests in Íszkéde, it should not ‒ cannot ‒ fail."** She clenched her fist, and another quiet sigh left her lips as her eyes began to discolour, only making Petra's worries worsen. **"Oceana is a host to life, is it not?"** Maria asked, her brows furrowing while her eyes averted from her clenched fist to Petra's very own eyes. *"Theoretically, it can.."* She mumbled back, shaking her head, attempting to shake off the idea of having lost *Glory* not even a minute ago. *"It's purely theoretical. Pond-1 found remains of cellular life. We haven't found a follow-up, though, that's what Honour and Glory intend to achieve."* \
Maria's eyes drifted off to the various consoles and monitors on the bridge to her right, and glancing out of the windows behind them, she leaned against the desk. **"It's beyond my grasp, still."** She sighed, letting go of the pressure on her clenched fist, and closing her eyes, the discolouration remained, similarly to how the streaks of grey also remained in her hair. *"I will contact Glory."* Petra interrupted Maria, disturbing her trains of thoughts, and surprised, she reopened her eyes in an instant, returning to focus without a second thought. *"Monitor Honour for me. The crew list is available there. Inform Chiaris as soon as you can about Glory's status."* \
Maria nodded, and immediately took a hold of the sheets of paper to her side. One stack of papers housed Glory's crew list, the other was a copy of Honour's crew, and flying through it, her eyes landed on a name all too familiar to her. Sorted alphabetically by the first name of every crew member, Maria froze upon reading the name that stood below Chiaris's. **"Cisila.."** She muttered under her breath, her offhand anchored to the desk once more. Her eyes opened wide, and without a single second thought, she took one of many transmitters at the desk and turned towards Petra. **"I'm contacting Chiaris. If necessary, I will carry the 100 or 230 to Oceana-1 for support by any means."** She almost yelled. Her voice was stern, and every word that left her lips was clearly said with intent. A promise she'd keep, no matter how complex it could even become, and with her eyes glowing brightly, her arms soon followed, the intricate pattern on her back becoming clearly visible, as if her coat wasn't even there to cover it. Stepping past Petra, the teardrop-shaped Halo formed behind her back, tidally locked to her shoulder blades, materialising from thin air. Her intentions were clear as day. **"I will *not* allow *any* lives to be lost."**
