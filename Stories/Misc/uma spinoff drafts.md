## Iron Lung x Dream of March
*// Setting: It's summer camp at the coast. Both the Classic and Senior years merged to train together, and even retired runners made their appearance to guide and aid the young up-and-coming racers in their journey to improve themselves in any way possible. For March, that improvement lies mostly in her mental. Lucky for her, a retired Crown runner had been keeping an eye on her, and as March sits on a bench while the sun begins to set at the beach, Iron Lung sits beside her and begins a chat with her.*

*"Mind if I sit here?"* A slightly deep, but warm voice gently called out beside her, and snapping out of her trance, March's ears perked upright in an instant and directed themselves towards the direction of the voice, soon followed by her eyes as she opened them wide in surprise. **"You're—No.. it's fine.."** She sighed back, her mind troubled—absent, and it took no time for her to return her eyes to the orange sand at her feet lit up by the setting sun before the two of them. March undoubtedly recognised who had just sat down beside her. *Iron Lung*, a crown runner from a few years back, who ran fourteen races, winning five including her debut. But next to that, she was also a frequent podium sitter, taking home silver five times. It was reminiscent of March's career so far, but she herself had no trophies yet to be proud of. *"What's bothering you?"* Lung casually said, her legs stretched out in front, heels dug into the sand. *"Is it what I think it is?"* \
March simply nodded, her eyes still fixated on the sand before her, her ears drooped to the side. **"Yeah.."** She sighed, her head full yet empty at the same time. Everywhere she looked, she saw Tide's back, her navy blue racewear always one length ahead of her. The chains at her waist waving in the wind, leaving March always within touching distance, but never ahead. No matter which strategy she ran, she would always find herself staring at Tide in the distance. If she ran in front, Tide would simply outrun her at the final stretch. If she dictated the pace, Tide would still find her way past. And even if she ran between the pace and observed, she could never match Tide's acceleration on the final stretch. She felt like she lacked, like she could never reach the heights that Tide was approaching. She was always one length behind. The Asahi Hai, the Oka Sho, the Japanese Oaks.. \
Lung rested her arms over the backrest on the bench, and with ears perked upright, her eyes scanned March's poor posture. She was leaning forwards, her hands uneasily on her thighs, and her eyes finally averted away from the sand, away from Lung. *"What's going through your mind?"* Lung asked, hoping to pick up a conversation that could revitalise March's spirits. After all, it was Olivia herself that personally asked Lung to do so. \
**"A lot.."** March sighed, her eyes once more looking down at the sand beneath her. Her tail swayed uneasily behind her back, picking up a thin layer of sand into the air. **"Everywhere I look, she's there. Always ahead of me, always in front. If I lead, she goes past me. If she leads, I can never catch up."** She inhaled deeply before continuing as her voice continued to get shakier with every word.
